What to settle with Bennett

  • Lock the unit price. Ask Bennett to hold $2.03/sf for every phase, or tie increases to a published index. Their terms let them reprice when a quantity is cut more than 10%, and one phase is only 8–12% of the job.
  • Price the extras. Milling at the swale between the two buildings and extra leveling asphalt ($130/ton over low or unstable base) are not in these numbers.
  • Confirm the phase lines. Areas are scaled from the yard outline to the 928,000 sf on the estimate. Walk them with Bennett before the first phase.
